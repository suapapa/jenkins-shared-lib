Build one esp-rs chip target, optionally produce a flash `.bin`, archive ELF(+bin) as tar.gz, and write a SHA256 checksum under `dist/`.

Required: <code>chip</code>, <code>binName</code>. Also needs <code>releaseTag</code> or <code>env.RELEASE_TAG</code>.

<pre><code>script {
  espRsBuild(chip: 'esp32s3', binName: 'my-firmware')
}
</code></pre>
